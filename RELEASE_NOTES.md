## NewTube+ 1.0

First release of this fork of NewTube (a phone-only SmartTube).

### Install
1. Download the APK that matches your phone. Most modern phones are **arm64-v8a**.
2. Open it and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/files app when Android asks.

This is a debug-signed build, so Play Protect may warn you; it installs alongside the regular NewTube.

### Highlights
- Frosted-glass top and bottom bars, Samsung One UI-style wave progress bar (music), accent-gradient progress bars
- Autoplay previews under feed videos, with a speaker toggle
- Black-bar detection and crop, plus a manual "Detect black bars" / "Undo crop" in the gear menu
- Smooth mini player, tab scroll memory, new app icon and splash
- Build of commit fd41b96

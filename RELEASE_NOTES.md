## NewTube+ 1.2.0

### Install
Download the APK for your phone: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every modern phone; **universal** works on any. Allow "Install unknown apps" when Android asks. It installs over earlier NewTube+ versions and keeps your settings; in the app, Settings → About → Check for updates finds it too.

### New
- **Feed previews play on into the video page.** Tap a playing preview and it keeps going while the page opens, its sound fades in, and the full-quality video takes over at the same moment.
- **A new wave progress bar,** in the style of BitChord Dial: three soft layers of colour that brighten where they cross, and a white scrubber with a coloured centre.
- **Playlist pages wear the glass look:** the back arrow and the playlist name float over the list as glass pills, like Home's.
- **Predictive back on playlist and Search pages:** the page blurs and fades as you swipe back, with the screen beneath showing through.
- **The video page comes back** after Android closed the app in the background, paused where you left it.
- **Swipe up on the video grows it further,** and you can still change your mind and slide it back down.

### Changed
- **The player opens with its controls hidden;** tap the video to show them.
- **Black bars are no longer detected automatically.** "Detect black bars" in the player's settings still crops them when you ask.

### Fixed
- No more red loading circle flashing while a preview hands over to the video page, or during a moment's stall.
- A card you opened from its preview gets its thumbnail back instead of keeping a frame of the video.
- Thumbnails no longer reload with a dark flash when you come back from the Playlists tab.
- No dark circle flashing when you open a playlist.

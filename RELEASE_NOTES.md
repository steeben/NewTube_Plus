## NewTube+ 1.1.1

### Install
Download the APK for your phone: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every modern phone; **universal** works on any. Allow "Install unknown apps" when Android asks. It installs over NewTube+ 1.1.0 and 1.0.1 and keeps your settings; in the app, Settings → About → Check for updates finds it too.

### Fixed
- **The scrubber stays at the end when a video ends** and pauses there; it used to jump back a few seconds. Play starts the video again.
- **The status bar fades to black as the mini player opens,** instead of turning black at once at the end.

New in 1.1.0: Liquid glass by default, the pill mini player, and more. See the [1.1.0 release](https://github.com/steeben/NewTube_Plus/releases/tag/v1.1.0).

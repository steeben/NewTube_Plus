## NewTube+ 1.1.0

### Install
Download the APK for your phone: **arm64-v8a** fits almost every modern phone; **universal** works on any. Allow "Install unknown apps" when Android asks. It installs over NewTube+ 1.0.1 and keeps your settings; in the app, Settings → About → Check for updates finds it too.

### What's new
- **Liquid glass is the default look:** floating glass pills for the top row and the navigation, with a lens that slides between tabs. Settings → Appearance → Glass offers Liquid, Frosted or Solid. (Coming from 1.0.1 with Frosted glass on, you get Liquid glass; with it off, Solid.)
- **Pill mini player, now the default:** the minimized video floats above the navigation with its own progress line, and slides between the player and the pill.
- **The navigation tucks in while you scroll down,** and comes back when you scroll up.
- **Your profile picture on the You tab.**
- **App icon: Glass or Flat,** in your accent colour (Settings → Appearance). The logo and splash screen follow the accent too.
- **Swipe up on the video to go fullscreen:** it grows a little under your finger and goes fullscreen when you let go.
- **Feed autoplay** plays up to 720p without stalling, and its time badge counts down.
- **Playlist and search pages** get the same glass navigation and mini player as Home.
- From NewTube 1.16–1.17.1: 1080p for vertical videos, vertical videos go fullscreen upright, channel avatars on feed cards, a quick double tap on play/pause, and a decoder failure no longer lowers your saved quality.

### Fixed
- Glass no longer breaks after pulling a list past its top, and the navigation icons stay put.
- The mini player no longer hides behind the navigation on playlist and search pages.
- The progress line follows the video when it shrinks into the floating card.
- The selected tab's highlight stays inside the tucked-in navigation.
- No scroll bar on Home, and many smaller animation fixes.
